## English
KAI (the "App") is a home-screen launcher. **The App does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data.**

- The App requests **no permissions** and has **no internet access**; it contains no ads, analytics or tracking SDKs.
- To show your apps, the App reads the list of installed apps that have a launcher icon, locally on your device. This list never leaves the device.
- Your settings (theme, dock, hidden apps, layouts, focus timer) are stored only in the App's private storage on your device. Uninstalling the App deletes them.
- The App does not read your notifications, messages, contacts, location, photos or files.
